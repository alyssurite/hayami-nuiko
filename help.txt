\[ `HELP v\.6\.0` *:* `2024\.06\.19` \]

*/help* — show this very message\.

*/channel* — enter *setting channel mode*\. Before entering this mode you __*have to*__ add this bot to *your channel*\. Also, you __*have to*__ let the bot send new posts\.

Then, in this mode you should forward a message from __*your channel*__ \(*do not forward other channels' posts\!*\) to this chat\. This way bot *will remember* your channel\.

_*Any text message*_ — bot will ignore it, unless there's a twitter or pixiv link in it, that could be parsed\.

If you send _*several*_ links at once in one message, *all twitter links will be parsed*, but __*pixiv links will not*__\.

\[ `TOGGLES` \]

*/forward* \(_default_: *off*\) — toggle forwarding messages to your channel\. Turn off for saving content\.

*/reply* \(_default_: *on*\) — toggle replying to your messages with the same content that will be posted to your channel\.

*/media* \(_default_: *off*\) — toggle replying to the post with twitter video*/gif*, if there's one\.

\[ `OTHER COMMANDS` \]

*/pixiv\_style* — cycle pixiv post styles\. Only *6 styles* are available\.

*/twitter\_style* — cycle twitter post styles\. Only *4 styles* are available\.

*/cancel* — cancel current operation\. *Use it in cases when you want to shut this bot up*\.

*/info* \[post link\] — get info about specified post from database\.

*/delete* \[post link\] — delete specified post in *your* channel from database and channel\.
